The promise is enforced by the chain.

## Tagline options
- Bonded settlement. Real money. No trust required.
- Solvers quote. The chain enforces.
- Real BTC arrives. The bond keeps them honest.

## Color Direction
- Primary: Deep navy (#0A1628)
- Accent: Metallic gold (#C9A227)
- Background: Near-black navy
- Text: Off-white

## Logo
Abstract interlocking shield / binding form in navy and gold.  
Symbolizes protection that is also a binding agreement.

## Voice
- Precise, not hype.
- Technical confidence without jargon walls.
- Focus on enforcement and recipient experience.

## Do / Don't
- Do: show the bond state, the Chain Signature tx, and the slash.
- Don't: claim “trustless everything” without showing the mechanism.
- Don't: hide the fact that this is built on NEAR Intents + Chain Signatures.
